Academic Profile

Overview & Philosophy

Pioneering secure, explainable, and resilient intelligence in Natural Language Processing

I am a Computer Science researcher and faculty member holding a Ph.D. in Information Technology from Delhi Technological University (DTU). My primary research is situated at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Adversarial Machine Learning, and Trustworthy AI.

As deep language models and Generative AI systems integrate into high-stakes domains, understanding their failure modes, vulnerabilities to adversarial perturbations, and safety boundaries is paramount. My lab investigates systematic stress-testing, character- and semantic-level perturbation attacks, defense mechanisms, and transparent model evaluation frameworks.

Core Research Interests

  • Robust & Trustworthy NLP: Vulnerability assessment & adversarial resilience.
  • Adversarial Machine Learning: Black-box & white-box attacks and defensive distillation.
  • LLM Safety & AI Reliability: Hallucination mitigation, jailbreak auditing, guardrails.
  • Explainable & Responsible AI (XAI): Post-hoc interpretability and feature attribution.
  • Transformer-based Model Evaluation: Out-of-distribution stress testing.

Pedagogy & Teaching

  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning: Foundations to state-of-the-art.
  • Deep Learning & Neural Architectures: CNNs, RNNs, Attention & Transformers.
  • Natural Language Processing: Language modeling, sentiment analysis, semantic search.
  • Data Structures & Algorithms: Algorithm design, time-space optimization.
  • Modern Programming: Python (PyTorch, HuggingFace, NumPy) & C++.
